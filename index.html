01 / The project

From video to understandable observations

FormAI Live is a learning and technology prototype. It samples frames from an exercise video, estimates body landmarks using a pretrained MediaPipe model and derives approximate two-dimensional joint measurements. The goal is interpretable feedback, not a clinical or professional assessment.

Input

Exercise video

Users select an exercise and upload a video using a browser interface.

Processing

Pose landmarks

The app checks visibility and calculates approximate 2D joint angles.

Output

Exploratory feedback

Users see observations with explicit accuracy and safety limitations.

04 / Responsible development

Limitations matter

FormAI Live is experimental. Its rules have not been clinically or professionally validated. Results depend on video quality, framing and visibility. The app does not diagnose injuries, guarantee technique safety or replace qualified professional guidance.
